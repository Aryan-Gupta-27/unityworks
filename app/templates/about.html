About UnityWorks

One workspace for a scattered study life.

Students already stitch their days together from messaging apps, cloud folders, note tools, and a task list nobody updates. UnityWorks is the attempt to put that loop in one place: identity, community, conversation, and the work itself.

Phase 1 is the foundation — accounts, roles, profiles, and a dashboard you can trust. Phase 2 is the social layer: discover people, join a community, discuss, and message. Later phases sit on the same models, the same navigation, and the same glass.

  • {{ icon('users') }} Communities with owners, moderators, and members.
  • {{ icon('chat') }} Private messages, unread counts, and history.
  • {{ icon('bell') }} A notification center invitations and comments can share.
  • {{ icon('search') }} Search across people, communities, projects, resources, and questions.
